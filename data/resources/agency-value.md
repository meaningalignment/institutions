# Philosophy of agency & value

What values are, how people choose when they conflict, and what it means to act with integrity: the picture of human agency that any attempt to align AI with human values rests on.

## Selected papers

- [What are human values, and how do we align AI to them?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10636)
  - Authors: Oliver Klingefjord, Ryan Lowe, Joe Edelman
  - Year: 2024
  - Type: Preprint
  - Summary: Builds on Charles Taylor and Ruth Chang to define values as the criteria people attend to when making meaningful choices, and uses that definition to elicit a moral graph from a representative sample of Americans.

- [Geist in the Machine](https://aeon.co/essays/if-we-hope-to-build-artificial-souls-where-should-we-start)
  - Authors: Pete Wolfendale
  - Year: 2026
  - Venue: Aeon
  - Type: Essay
  - Summary: Rejects both the rationalist view that intelligence is calculation and the romantic view that mind cannot be mechanized, and draws on Kant and Hegel to treat freedom, analyzed as wisdom, creativity, and autonomy, as what an artificial agent would need.

- [Beyond Preferences in AI Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16984)
  - Authors: Tan Zhi-Xuan, Micah Carroll, Matija Franklin, Hal Ashton
  - Year: 2024
  - Venue: Philosophical Studies
  - Type: Peer-reviewed
  - Summary: Shows why preference satisfaction is too thin a target for alignment and develops a broader account of what AI systems should respond to.
